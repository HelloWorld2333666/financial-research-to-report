Where each colour goes

ElementTreatment
HeadingsCharcoal serif title (28 pt); warm-grey tracker above; charcoal hairline under the title block. Never burgundy titles.
ChartsThe focus country in wine (2.75 pt line, every point marked); peers in peer greys; gap brackets and leaders in antique gold; gridlines barely visible.
TablesCharcoal header over a charcoal rule; hairline rows; the focus row on wine tint with a wine bar; a worse change in wine, a better one in grey.
KPI calloutsThe lead figure in wine serif; supporting figures in charcoal serif; captions in warm grey; context figures in gold.
Diagrams and matricesNumbered wine badges; focus-country figures in wine serif, peer figures in charcoal; hairline rows; the conclusion under a heavier charcoal rule.
Section dividersDeep-wine field; the numeral and a short rule in antique gold; the section title in ivory serif.
