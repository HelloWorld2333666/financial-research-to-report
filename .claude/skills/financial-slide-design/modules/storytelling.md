# Storytelling and consulting logic

**Principles:** ST1–ST5.

**Three levels of message on every slide:**
1. The action title states the conclusion.
2. The exhibit headers state the sub-findings.
3. The panel states what it means for the audience.

**Every sentence is computed** from the evidence, and the brief refuses data that would make it false.

**Deck rhythm:**
- an executive summary first;
- an agenda;
- numbered dividers;
- content that changes composition from slide to slide;
- no focal figure repeated on adjacent slides (ST4, planned as a QA check).

**Wording:**
- **State directions and ranks,** not adjectives.
- **Show then and now together** (ST2).
- **Use a ratio statement as an anchor** only when the ratio is a recorded derivation (ST3).
