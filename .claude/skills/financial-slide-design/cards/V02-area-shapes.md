**Purpose:** a striking gist of a ratio, when the exact value matters less than "how much bigger".

**Applies when:**
- two values (growth against trend; a spread now against its history);
- or a part within its whole;
- often on statement pages.

**Hard constraints:** **area, not radius or side**, is proportional to the value.

**Heuristics:** a few values with large differences. Similar values make the shapes useless.

**Roles:** the visual anchor of a statement page; a companion to a ratio figure.

**Combines with:** A1 ratio statement ("~2x"), P6 full-bleed statement, V10 (squares placed on regions).

**Risks:**
- **Works when** the ratio itself is the message.
- **Fails when** readers need precise comparison (area is under-perceived): use bars.

**Execution:**
- Nested circles share a bottom or centre anchor.
- The inner circle is in the focal tone, the outer in a lighter step of the same ramp (R2).
- Values are labelled directly (M5).

**Variants:**
- **T08:** nested circles (part within whole; two rates).
- **T09:** proportional squares side by side.

**Alternatives:** bars; an A1 ratio statement alone.
