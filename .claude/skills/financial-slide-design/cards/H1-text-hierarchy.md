**Purpose:** make the reading order show what matters.

**Levels:**
1. **Action title (T79):** one conclusion sentence that states a direction or comparison. *Target:* about 30–36 pt; shorten the sentence before shrinking the type.
2. **Exhibit header (T80):** the sub-finding in bold, with the unit and period on the next line in regular weight at the same size, then a rule or space.
3. **Commentary:** short blocks opening with a bold lead-in of a few words (words only, no figures in the lead-in).
4. **Conclusions (T34):** one primary insight in the strongest treatment; supporting observations quieter, smaller or lower. Avoid identical boxes of identical weight.
5. **Numbered sequences (T66):** large numerals or numbered circles for stages, levers and recommendations.

**Three message levels** (ST1): the title states the conclusion; exhibit headers state the sub-findings; the panel or conclusion states the implication.

**Hard constraints:**
- Legible at the rendered size.
- Body text about 12 pt or more where possible (R6).
- Figures in text are tokens, bound.

**Execution:**
- Few levels with strong contrast (weight before size).
- Key phrases bold, or in the highlight colour, sparingly.
