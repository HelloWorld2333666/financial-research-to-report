**Purpose:** set the subject and identity on navigation pages through layered imagery, typography and texture.

**Applies when:** covers, section dividers, closings, occasionally a statement page.

**Layers:**
1. a background (white, solid or gradient);
2. a texture (S2 line pattern);
3. a subject image (S2: cutout, crop or duotone, recoloured to the palette);
4. the title and short text;
5. numbering (H1).

**Combines with:** S2 imagery and linework, P5 title field, H1 numbered levers.

**Hard constraints:** images carry a recorded source and licence; nothing data-like is generated.

**Risks:**
- **Works when** the image is the subject (steel for a steel deck, equipment for a medical one) and integrated with the palette.
- **Fails when:**
  - a crude pictogram is enlarged (R12);
  - an unrelated stock photo is used;
  - the image fights the title.

**Execution:**
- Crop by the slide edge.
- Recolour to the ramp.
- The title on calm space.
- Layers don't compete for the focal point (R1, R8).
