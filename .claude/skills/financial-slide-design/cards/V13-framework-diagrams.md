**Purpose:** two dimensions at once, such as category × level of ambition, for strategic options and policy levers.

**Applies when:** many items (levers, measures) fall into a few categories and an ordered level.

**Hard constraints:**
- Categories and levels are real.
- Any highlighted coverage reflects the stated plan.

**Variants:**
- **T51 radial framework:**
  - categories as arcs;
  - items as wedges;
  - an axis of ambition;
  - category icons.
- **T52 framework comparison:** two T51 side by side, each with:
  - a highlighted outline overlay (M1 outline);
  - ghosted context;
  - arrow-shaped banner headers (chevrons).

**Heuristics:** enough items to fill the framework; few items look empty.

**Roles:** main exhibit; with P5 title-led (a title block beside the framework).

**Combines with:** S1 icons in category arcs; M1 outline and ghosting; G1 chevron banners.

**Risks:**
- **Works when** both dimensions matter.
- **Fails when:**
  - there are too few items;
  - the labels are unreadable at their rotation;
  - the axis is decorative.

**Execution:**
- Category arcs in distinct steps of the ramp; wedges light with white gutters (R3).
- Labels rotated along radii.
- The highlight as a thin outline in a separate semantic colour (R2).

**Alternatives:** V09 matrix (categories × levels); a grouped list.
