**Always on:**
- **Source line (T84a)** on every slide that shows data: dataset codes and retrieval dates.
- **Page numbers (T83)** on every slide except the cover.
- **A light frame (LY1):** no heavy band; the title high; the source at the foot.

**Conditional:**
- **Numbered footnotes (T81):** superscript markers on labels, notes above the source, for definitions, breaks in series and estimates.
- **Caveat tags (T82):** "Illustrative", "Not exhaustive", "Directional", set small beside the exhibit header.

**Mechanical QA (QA2), hard checks:**
- no overflow, overlap or out-of-bounds elements;
- type at or above the minimum;
- integrity passes with no unsourced or stale figures;
- every data mark is named.

**Planned additions:**
- perceptibility of the message-carrying difference;
- label room (treemap tiles, segments);
- area encoding;
- text contrast.
