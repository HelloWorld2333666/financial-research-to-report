**Purpose:** order through time or stages, with the geometry that matches the relation.

**Choosing the geometry:**
- **T21 timeline:** real dates; events placed on a dated line.
- **T47 serpentine journey:** staged path, three to six stages, with large numerals and alternating text.
- **T48 arc or curved process:** stages that build or escalate.
- **T49 cycle:** **only for a genuinely recurring loop.** A one-way process is not forced into a circle without justification.

**Hard constraints:**
- The geometry represents the relation.
- Dates are real.
- Figures are bound.

**Heuristics:** short text per stage; stage icons from one set.

**Roles:** main exhibit; illustrative example pages ("Illustrative" caveat, C1).

**Combines with:**
- H1 numerals (T66);
- S1 icons;
- S2 illustrations in rings (T75);
- a colour transition along the ramp (R2).

**Risks:**
- **Works when** the shape adds meaning (return, escalation, stages).
- **Fails when** geometry is used for decoration, or text crowds the path.

**Execution:**
- One continuous path in graded tones.
- Numerals large.
- Stage text aligned to the path side.

**Alternatives:** a numbered list (H1); V07 with event markers.

**Example:** among the references, only the serpentine journey has one (u05). No timeline, arc or cycle example seen.
