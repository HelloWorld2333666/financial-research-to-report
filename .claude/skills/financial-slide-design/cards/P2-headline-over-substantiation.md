**Purpose:** the message first, the evidence second. A top tier carries the headline proportions or figures; a lower tier substantiates them.

**Applies when:** two or three key shares or figures, plus a breakdown or extra measures for the same entities.

**Regions and roles:**
- **headline:** V01 rings, A1 figures, V02 shapes or a small chart;
- **substantiation:** V09 table, V05 or V04.

**Worked combination T02:** two rings, each with a centre figure and a small title, over a table breaking the shares down.

**Hard constraints:** both tiers come from the same evidence; the headline figures appear in the table, or are derived from it.

**Combines with:** V01, A1, V09, M1 (the focal row tinted), H1.

**Risks:**
- **Works when** the tiers answer "what" and "why or which".
- **Fails when** the tiers repeat the same numbers, or the headline has no clear subject.

**Execution:**
- The headline tier has the larger visual weight but the smaller area.
- Aligned left edges and a shared header system (R10).

**Example:** u01 has rings as the headline (not over a table). **No donut-over-table example has been seen.**
