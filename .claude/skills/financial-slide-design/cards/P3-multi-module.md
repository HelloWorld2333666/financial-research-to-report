**Purpose:** complementary evidence on one page. Each module answers one sub-question, with its own finding-led header.

**Applies when:** two to four sub-claims that together support the title.

**Regions and roles:**
- **modules:** each a family suited to its sub-question;
- **optional conclusion or anchors.**

**Variants:**
- **Dashboard:** parallel modules, sized by their weight.
- **Zoom chain (T26):** general → national ranking → local map, with headers that read as a chain.
- **Small-multiple page:** V08 as the page.

**Combines with:** any V families (a share, a comparison, a mix, as in u01); D1 join symbols; G1 light dividers; H1 headers.

**Risks:**
- **Works when** each module uses the form its sub-question needs, stripped to essentials (R11).
- **Fails when:**
  - modules are equal thirds regardless of weight;
  - they repeat each other;
  - each carries full chart chrome.

**Execution:**
- Module sizes follow their importance.
- Shared baselines and header rules.
- Light vertical dividers or whitespace (R3, R10).
