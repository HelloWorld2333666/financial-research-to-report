**Purpose:** a pause in the deck that carries one comparison, one map or one ratio across the whole page.

**Applies when:**
- a statement (a doubling, a regional definition);
- a single striking comparison between evidence-dense pages.

**Regions and roles:** title; one visual (V02, V10, A1); a caveat or source.

**Combines with:** V02 nested shapes, A1 ratio statement, V10 map with symbols, C1 caveat tag.

**Risks:**
- **Works when** the page has one idea.
- **Fails when** dense evidence is forced onto a dark full-bleed page.

**Execution:**
- **A light or dark field** (R13).
- On dark: white or light-accent type, with the data in the brightest steps of the ramp.
- Generous scale for the single visual (R1).
